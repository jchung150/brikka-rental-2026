**Subject:** BRIKKA — Change Order No. 1 for your confirmation

Dear [Name],

Please find attached Change Order No. 1 to the Software Development Agreement of
August 24, 2026 and its Statement of Work.

Over the past several weeks the functional specification has been rewritten for commercial
building leasing. The specification named as the baseline in the SOW describes a residential
system and is no longer a usable basis for development, so the contract documents need to
catch up with what is actually being built. This Change Order records that, together with the
consequent changes to the delivery phases and the schedule.

The substantive changes are:

- **Baseline documents.** The residential functional specification is replaced by the BRIKKA
  Functional Specification v1.5 dated October 7, 2026. The Figma link is replaced by the
  interactive mock-up.
- **Delivery phases.** The work is divided into Phase 1 — the administrative back-office,
  35 screens — and Phase 2, covering the tenant and landlord portals, the notification centre,
  the landing page and the virtual account payment gateway. Phase 2 will be scheduled and
  priced separately.
- **Schedule.** Stage 1 delivery stays at November 13, 2026. Phase 1 final delivery moves from
  January 15 to February 12, 2027, with inspection running to February 26 and the contract
  period ending February 28, 2027.
- **Fee.** The total fee of CAD $42,000 is unchanged and applies to Phase 1 in full. The advance
  of CAD $21,000 has been received; the balance of CAD $21,000 falls due after final acceptance
  of Phase 1. No payment arises on acceptance of the Stage 1 deliverables.
- **Terminology.** The Agreement uses "Phase" in two different senses — a stage of work in §1,
  and a division of functionality in SOW §7. Section 0 separates these into "Stage" and "Phase"
  so that the milestones are unambiguous at inspection.

The revised dates assume a development capacity of 120 hours per month. Section 4.3 provides for
re-baselining if the screen-level estimate that follows acceptance of the database design turns
out to be inconsistent with them.

Three decisions remain open on your side and are recorded in Section 5: the legal review of
personal information handling, requested on September 24; the tax treatment of corrected tax
invoices on credit adjustments; and whether rent and management-fee bank accounts are operated
separately. The first of these feeds directly into the database schema, so an early answer is
the most useful. Under Section 2.3 of the Agreement, delivery dates extend day for day while
these remain outstanding.

The document carries no signature block. A reply confirming your agreement will serve as the
written agreement required under Section 4 of the Agreement, and we will keep it on file with
the Change Order.

Please let me know if anything needs adjusting.
